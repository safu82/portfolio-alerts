## 1. What Happened This Week

Nifty 50 shed **-0.5%** over the week, drifting from **24,366** on Monday's open reference to **24,252** by Friday, with a mid-week gut-check on **Wednesday's close at 24,078** before a modest two-day recovery. The dominant theme was risk-off from a **twin macro squeeze**: **Brent crude ripped +7.6% to $93.74** on US-Iran tensions and a US Senate sanctions bill targeting buyers of Russian crude, while the **RBI's MPC minutes** (Monetary Policy Committee — the rate-setting body's meeting notes) came out hawkish, prompting a bond sell-off and rate-sensitive sector weakness. Layer in a fresh **10% US "safeguard" tariff on India** confirmed by the White House, a **₹3/litre petrol-diesel hike** at home, and a **100% sugar import-duty waiver**, and you have a week where policy did most of the moving. The recovery on **Thursday (+153 points)** looks like short-covering rather than conviction — breadth stayed weak.

## 9. The Bottom Line

1. **The market is coiled, not trending.** Nifty pinned between EMA50 and EMA200, VCP setups expanding while momentum signals contract, 54% of stocks above 200-EMA, and volatility subdued (VIX 10.76). This is a compression setup — the next macro headline (Brent, tariff clarification, or a fresh RBI signal) likely picks the direction.
2. **IT weakness looks macro, not fundamental** — TCS/Infy/HCL all posted decent June-quarter numbers but got sold on the 10% US tariff. If the tariff softens in trade talks, the risk-reward may re-rate; if it hardens or expands, IT has further to fall. Worth watching, not front-running.
3. **The banking quadrant flip to Leading is the most interesting rotation** — but it's a private-bank story (Kotak +3%, HDFC Bank flat) not a full sector call. PSU-bank weakness on hawkish RBI minutes is the risk that could break the sector back down. Caveat: RBI MPC minutes signalled "impending hikes" per Reuters — if that materialises, the banking-leading thesis needs a rethink.
