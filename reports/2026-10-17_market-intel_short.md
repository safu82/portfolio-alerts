## The Week in One Line
Nifty rallied **+2.8%** to 23,146 as IT stocks led a sharp rebound, helped by a stronger rupee and softer global oil-sanction worries.

## What's Working

- **IT (+5.4%)**: Big names like TCS, HCLTECH, WIPRO and TECHM jumped 13–18% over five days after solid Sep-quarter results. The rupee stabilising near 97/USD also helps IT exporters earn more in rupee terms.
- **Agriculture (+5.1%)**: Rural themes caught a bid alongside the IMF's upgrade of India's growth outlook to 7% for FY27.
- **Media (+3.7%)**: A quiet bounce from oversold levels — the sector is still deep in the red over 6 months, so this is more a dead-cat recovery than a trend change.

## What's Not

- **ETF (-3.4%)**: This mostly reflects Indian **gold and silver ETFs** falling as global silver dropped nearly 5% and the gold/silver ratio widened. A pullback after a very strong year (ETF basket is still +24.7% over 12 months).
- **Aviation (-3.3%)**: Brent crude rose ~1.8% to $105 — higher fuel costs squeeze airline margins. INDIGO was among the week's biggest rank decliners.
- **Healthcare (-1.2%)**: Profit-taking after a strong 6-month run (+9.5%). No single bad catalyst, just rotation out.

## Notable Events

**Trump waives US sanctions on Russian oil — India exempted.** This is big for India's import bill. Russian crude is roughly a third of India's oil imports, and the removal of tariff risk means refiners (and the rupee) get breathing room. Bullish for energy downstream names and the broader macro.

**RBI steps up rupee defence.** The central bank introduced curbs on forward FX contracts to stop speculation against the rupee, which had hit record lows. Forex reserves fell $52bn in a month — a sign the RBI is spending heavily to defend the currency. The rupee stabilised near 97/USD, which is good news for IT and pharma exporters' reported earnings and for importers' input costs.

**IMF upgrades India's growth to 7%** for FY27 and FY28 — the largest upgrade among major emerging markets. Deloitte echoed with 7.1–7.3%. This kind of top-down endorsement often brings foreign inflows back after weeks of selling.

## Charts to Know

**The sector rotation map flipped sharply toward IT.** Picture a four-quadrant chart where sectors move between "Leading," "Weakening," "Lagging," and "Improving" based on their strength vs. the index. IT jumped from "Improving" straight into "Leading" this week — the strongest kind of move. Meanwhile Industrial, Healthcare and Aviation all slid into "Lagging." The market isn't rising broadly; it's rotating hard into exporters.

**Breadth is lukewarm.** Breadth = how many stocks are rising vs. falling. Despite Nifty closing the week up strongly, Thursday actually saw more decliners (364) than advancers (218). Only **33% of stocks** trade above their 200-day average — meaning two-thirds of the market is still in a longer-term downtrend. The rally is real but narrow, driven by heavyweight IT names.

## What to Watch Next Week

- **Earnings**: Reliance Jio Financial impact, more banking names (Axis, ICICI, Kotak expected), and FMCG majors. Watch if the IT earnings strength spreads to mid-tier names.
- **Macro**: Rupee behaviour vs. 97/USD — any renewed weakness forces RBI's hand again. US inflation data and crude oil direction after the Russia sanction waiver matter for next week's open.
- **Nifty levels**: Nifty is **above its 20-day average (22,831)** — a short-term positive — but still **below its 50-day (23,278) and 200-day (24,018) averages**. Reclaiming 23,278 would be the next bullish signal; losing 22,831 would hand control back to the sellers.
