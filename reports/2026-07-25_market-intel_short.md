## The Week in One Line
Nifty slid **-2.3%** to 23,787.55 as a sharp spike in crude oil rattled sentiment and dragged the index below all major moving averages.

## What's Working

- **ETFs (+2.5%)** — A broad ETF basket led the tape, likely reflecting flows into gold and defensive/thematic ETFs as investors sought shelter from the equity sell-off.
- **Logistics (+0.7%)** — Held up on the back of strong Jun-quarter earnings (Mahindra Logistics posted EPS growth of +335%), suggesting operating leverage is finally kicking in.
- **Agriculture (+0.3%)** — A quiet, defensive winner. This sector often outperforms when the broader market wobbles.

## What's Not

- **Aviation (-4.7%)** — Brent crude jumped **+17.8%** in a week to $100. Jet fuel is airlines' single biggest cost. IndiGo also swung to a loss (EPS -111% YoY), a double blow.
- **Realty (-3.9%)** — After a strong month, profit-booking hit hard. Rising crude also stokes inflation fears, which pushes back rate-cut hopes — bad news for interest-rate-sensitive property stocks.
- **Conglomerate (-3.5%)** — Reliance's -22% EPS drop and -3.7% price fall over five days weighed on the whole basket, given its heavy index weight.
- **IT (-2.6%)** — Despite decent earnings (TCS, Infy, HCL all in line), the sector remains in a downtrend (**-21% year-to-date**) as concerns about US client spending linger.

## Notable Events

**Brent crude surged nearly 18% in a week to $100.** This is the single biggest story. It hits aviation directly, squeezes margins for paint/tyre/chemical makers, and worsens India's import bill (we buy ~85% of our oil). It also feeds inflation, which complicates the RBI's rate-cut path.

**Banking heavyweights disappointed on price, not earnings.** HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank both grew EPS ~15-20%, but their stock prices fell **-9.4%** and **-0.8%** over five days respectively. Translation: results were fine, but the market wanted more — or is simply de-risking. HDFC Bank's rank slid sharply (now #479 of ~500), a notable weakness signal.

**Bright spot — capital-goods and select autos crushed it.** BHEL delivered **+182% EPS growth**, Bajaj Auto **+46%**, TVS Motor **+67%**. Both auto names actually gained 6-7% over five days despite the broader fall — the **Automobile sector flipped from Weakening to Leading** in relative-strength terms (a technical way of saying money is rotating in).

## Charts to Know

**Breadth turned ugly mid-week.** Breadth simply counts how many stocks went up vs down each day. On Monday/Tuesday, advances beat declines comfortably (345 vs 238, then 325 vs 255). But Wednesday and Thursday saw a wipeout — only **109 and 119 stocks advanced against 470+ declining**. That's a broad-based sell, not a rotation. Friday recovered slightly (313 up, 267 down), hinting the selling may be pausing.

**Stocks above their 200-day average slipped from 55% to 50%.** The 200-day moving average is the classic dividing line between "long-term uptrend" and "long-term downtrend." Half the market is now on the wrong side of it — a caution flag, though not a crisis.

## What to Watch Next Week

- **Earnings**: Most large-caps have reported. Watch the mid-cap earnings tail — particularly in **chemicals** (Guj Alkalies posted +498% EPS), **realty**, and **financial services**, where sector-wide EPS growth has been strong.
- **Macro**: **Crude oil is the swing factor.** Any further move above $100 pressures aviation, paints, and the rupee. Also watch DXY (dollar index at 101.43, up modestly) — a stronger dollar means more FII selling pressure.
- **Nifty levels**: The index is below its 20-day (24,072), 50-day (23,979), and 200-day (24,410) averages. **23,750 is immediate support**; a break opens up 23,500. On the upside, Nifty needs to reclaim **24,000** to signal the pullback is done.

Stay patient. Corrections like this are normal — the medium-term earnings picture (banking +27%, metals +113%, financials +26%) remains healthy.
