## 1. What Happened This Week

Nifty 50 closed the week at **24,570.65**, up **+0.9%** from Sunday's 24,363.35 — a modest headline number that hides a very active midweek. Monday saw a **+411-point surge** to 24,774 on lower crude and FII inflows, but the rest of the week gave back nearly half those gains as three domestic catalysts landed in sequence: the **RBI held rates at 5.25%** with a dovish tilt (inflation projected to peak at 5.9% in Q3 FY27), **petrol/diesel prices were hiked by ₹3/litre** (CNG in Delhi up ₹2), and the **US Senate passed a Russia sanctions bill** threatening 100% tariffs on Indian buyers of Russian oil. The theme this week isn't the index — it's the **rotation underneath it**: Auto Components, Aviation and Defence flipped into Leading, while Energy and Realty broke down.

Aviation still rallied +3.5% because the crude move dominated, but keep the domestic drag in mind.
- **DEFENCE: Lagging → Improving** (RS Δ +5.5). MIDHANI in the top-10 rank-surge list.
- **INFRASTRUCTURE: Lagging → Improving** (RS Δ +4.5). KNR Constructions, Man Infra, Engineers India all in the rank-surge top 10.

- **REALTY: Weakening → Lagging** (RS Δ -4.2). Rate-sensitive; RBI's pause without a hint of cuts disappointed.

The **ETF +6.0%** print in the top slot deserves flagging: gold rose +2.5% and silver +4.5% globally this week, but Indian precious-metal ETFs typically outrun those moves when there's a domestic tailwind. The data doesn't give us an explicit duty change, so read this cautiously — some of it is just the global bid.

## 9. The Bottom Line

1. **Rotation is doing the heavy lifting, not the index.** Nifty +0.9% masks an 11-sector quadrant reshuffle. Auto Components, Aviation, Defence and Infrastructure all flipped positive on the RRG; Energy and Realty broke down. The signal count expanding to 133 unique stocks (MACD Buy alone +20 WoW) says momentum is genuinely turning across a broader universe — but the multi-signal cluster is concentrated in auto-components and select mid-caps, so treat "broadening" with appropriate skepticism.

2. **Bajaj Finance's -5.5% on a +27% EPS is the most important tell of the week.** The market is pricing an NBFC compliance headwind that isn't in any reported number yet. If this read-through extends to the cohort next week, financial services sector momentum could turn — worth watching, not fading.

3. **The domestic-vs-global crude divergence sets up an uneven auto trade.** Brent -5% is a fundamental tailwind, but the ₹3/litre domestic fuel hike partially offsets it for aviation and logistics. Auto Components benefits more cleanly (input costs, not fuel costs). The rotation signal is real; the sector selection matters. Caveat: with 156 stocks still to report this quarter, sector medians could shift as the tail lands.
