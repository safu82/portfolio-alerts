## The Week in One Line
Nifty drifted up **+0.4%** to 23,746 in a choppy week dominated by RBI action, a weaker rupee, and easing crude.

## What's Working
- **IT (+3.8%)**: A weaker rupee helps exporters — software companies earn in dollars but report in rupees, so a falling rupee boosts profits. Infosys and TCS led the bounce after a brutal 6-month slide.
- **Aviation (+2.9%)**: Brent crude fell from $105 to $104.62 (-6% week). Fuel is roughly 30-40% of airline costs, so cheaper oil = fatter margins.
- **Realty (+1.9%)**: Hopes that the RBI won't hike interest rates kept home-loan-sensitive stocks alive. Prestige's blowout earnings (+902% EPS) added fuel.

## What's Not
- **Media (-3.1%)**: Continued weak ad spend and falling viewership. The sector is now down 28% over a year.
- **Logistics (-3.1%)**: A sharp rotation out — the sector flipped from Weakening to Lagging on the quadrant map (a tool that tracks which sectors lead or trail the market). Profit-booking after strong earnings.
- **FMCG (-1.4%)**: A double hit. ITC's results disappointed (EPS -73%), and the government is mulling a hike in edible oil import duty — which would raise input costs for makers of biscuits, snacks, and packaged food.

## Notable Events

**New RBI Governor.** Sanjay Malhotra was appointed Governor of the Reserve Bank of India — the central bank that sets interest rates and manages the rupee. Crucially, the RBI signalled it will **not rush a rate hike** despite the falling rupee, prioritising inflation control instead. That's growth-friendly and explains why rate-sensitive sectors (realty, autos to some extent) held up.

**RBI defends the rupee, announces $5bn swap.** The rupee hit fresh record lows on US-Iran tensions and a crude spike to $105. The RBI sold an estimated $2bn+ on Thursday to steady the currency, and announced a **$5 billion dollar-rupee swap auction** for May 26 — essentially a tool to inject rupee liquidity into the banking system. Banks and INR-sensitive sectors got a boost.

**Edible oil duty hike on the table.** The government is weighing a higher import duty on edible oils to support domestic farmers. If passed, FMCG companies face higher input costs — already visible in the sector's weakness this week.

## Charts to Know

**Breadth is mixed but improving.** Breadth measures how *many* stocks are rising vs falling — not just the index. Monday saw 461 stocks fall vs 126 rising (ugly). By Friday it was nearly even (294 up, 290 down). The **% of stocks above their 200-day moving average** — a long-term trend gauge — sits at just 44%. Translation: the rally is narrow. Less than half of stocks are in long-term uptrends.

**Nifty vs its moving averages.** A moving average smooths out daily noise to show the trend. Nifty closed below its 20-day (-0.2%), 50-day (-0.9%), and 200-day (-3.5%) averages. The index is range-bound below resistance, not in a clear uptrend yet.

## What to Watch Next Week

- **Earnings**: Torrent Pharma and Hindalco just reported; watch follow-through. The Mar 2026 season is largely done — focus shifts to management commentary on guidance.
- **Macro events**: The RBI's **$5 billion swap auction on May 26** — a market-moving liquidity event. Also watch crude oil (any US-Iran flare-up) and rupee levels.
- **Nifty levels**: Resistance at **23,793** (20-day average) and **23,950** (50-day average) — Nifty needs to cross these for any sustained rally. Support sits around **23,600**, this week's low. A break below could open the door to 23,400.
