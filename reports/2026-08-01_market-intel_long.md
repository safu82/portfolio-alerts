## 1. What Happened This Week

Nifty 50 climbed from **23,787.55 to 24,363.35 — a solid +2.4% week**, with the heaviest lifting done on Wednesday (+259 points) and momentum sustained through Friday. The dominant theme was **rotation into cyclicals and IT** — driven by a specific, identifiable macro cocktail: **Brent crude collapsed 12.5%** to $87.80, the **US placed India in the lower 10% tariff tier** with $87 billion of exports exempted from additional duties, and **strong June industrial data** built the case for Q1 FY27 GDP growth above 7%. The offsetting bearish catalysts — a **₹3/litre petrol-diesel price hike**, the US Senate advancing a Russia Sanctions Bill with a **100% tariff threat** on India for Russian oil imports, and inflation pressure visible in Hindustan Unilever's results — kept FMCG and consumer staples on the back foot. This is a market climbing on external tailwinds while quietly worrying about domestic consumption.

## 2. The Trend Picture

Nifty sits in a **mixed but improving posture**:

- **Above EMA 20** (Exponential Moving Average — a smoothed average of recent closes traders use as trend reference) at 24,080 — by +1.2%.
- **Above EMA 50** at 23,997 — by +1.5%.
- **Below EMA 200** at 24,426 — by -0.3%.

So the short and medium-term trend is up, but the long-term line remains the ceiling — Nifty is still working to reclaim it. Breadth confirms the improvement: **% of stocks above the 200-day EMA rose from 50% to 55%** over the week. The strongest single-day advance-decline (A/D — stocks up vs down) split came **Wednesday, 441 advancing vs 141 declining** — a textbook broad-participation up day that lined up with the +259-point Nifty jump. Fresh 52-week highs remained scarce (just 1 on Friday), and new lows collapsed from 10 to 2 — a classic "washout ending" pattern where selling exhausts itself. Trend is broadening, not narrowing.

## 4. Earnings Pulse — Jun 2026 Quarter

**246 stocks have reported; 297 haven't yet** — so we're roughly two-thirds through the season and can read patterns with some confidence.

**(a) Sector aggregate.** The quarter is broadly strong across cyclicals. **Logistics leads at +81% median EPS YoY** (small sample of 3), followed by **Metals +72%**, **Realty +67%**, and **Consumer Durables +56%**. Every one of the 18 tracked sectors has a positive median — no sector is in aggregate contraction. FMCG's headline +27.5% median is deceptive given the HUL and ITC softness discussed below.

**(b) The extremes** — colour only. Glenmark (+931%), Sapphire (+833%), HFCL (+777%) top the beats list, but these are low-base effects on smaller names — don't read them as sector signals. On the miss side, **HINDPETRO (-398%) and BPCL (-127%)** are the more meaningful data points because they cluster in energy refining — a signal that oil-marketing companies took a beating on inventory losses as crude fell.

**(c) Bellwether reads** — where the story really is:

- **Reliance: -22% EPS on +27% revenue, +2.3% for the week.** The market shrugged off the earnings miss because revenue growth is strong and the crude collapse actually helps Reliance's downstream. Not a sector-mover this week.
- **HDFC Bank: +18% EPS, -0.8% on print day, +0.7% for the week.** A muted response to a solid number — this is the RBI-differential-rates concern showing up in price. Read-through: PSU and private banks all likely under mild NIM pressure.
- **Bajaj Finance: +27% EPS, +12.7% for the week.** The week's cleanest bellwether signal. Big print, big reaction, and Bajaj Finserv (+8.1%) confirming. NBFC demand is genuinely accelerating.
- **Nestle: +48% EPS, +4.6% for the week** vs **HUL: -3% EPS, -2.0% for the week.** The FMCG split is stark. Premium/discretionary FMCG (Nestle) is holding up while mass-market staples (HUL) are getting squeezed by inflation. The HUL result is what the Financial Times called out and it's the read-through worth taking home.
- **Bajaj Auto: +46% EPS, +65% revenue, +3.5% for the week; M&M: +34% EPS, +7.5%.** The auto sector's +5.9% sector return is legit — the bellwethers are printing and the tape is confirming.

**Staleness caveat:** The "Recent Earnings Declared (last 10 days)" list is empty, meaning most of these bellwether prints landed earlier in the reporting window. The **current-week price action is rotation, not fresh earnings reaction** — with the possible exceptions of Bajaj Finance and BajajFinserv, where the 1-day price moves (+8.3% and +6.3%) suggest recent prints.

## 5. Technical Signals This Week

Total universe: **108 unique stocks** firing at least one signal. The mix is telling — **momentum indicators (MACD) expanded, breakout indicators (VCP, Blue Zone Strong, Bull Flag) contracted.**

- **Blue Zone Buy** (dashboard's institutional-accumulation watchlist: RSI strength + close above EMA20 + within 10% of 52W high): **45 stocks vs 58 prior week, -13.** The core accumulation universe is contracting — the rally is real but narrowing. Top examples: SONACOMS, KALYANKJIL, LAURUSLABS, QUESS, FEDERALBNK.
- **MACD Buy** (momentum oscillator turning positive — a trend-change signal): **31 vs 22, +9.** Trend changes are accelerating. LAURUSLABS, FEDERALBNK, LODHA topping the list.
- **MACD Strong**: **25 vs 16, +9.** Confirmation that MACD-based momentum is broadening. LODHA, QUESS, LAURUSLABS.
- **Narrow CPR Breakaway** (Central Pivot Range — a tight pivot setup often preceding directional breakouts): **29 vs 23, +6.** Coiling followed by expansion — bullish. TECHM, SUNPHARMA, ULTRACEMCO.
- **Golden Cross Buy** (20-day EMA crossing above 50-day EMA — medium-term bullish trigger): **7 vs 7, unchanged.** HEG, DALBHARAT, HEROMOTOCO.
- **VCP Buy** (Volatility Contraction Pattern — tightening range before a breakout): **4 vs 11, -7.** Breakout setups thinning meaningfully. TITAN, TECHM.
- **Blue Zone Strong**: **3 vs 7, -4.** SONACOMS, DIVISLAB.
- **Bull Flag Buy** (chart pattern: sharp rally, orderly consolidation, then breakout above the range): **2 vs 3, -1.** KALYANKJIL, QUESS.
- **Darvas Box**: **1 vs 1.** SUNPHARMA.

**Multi-signal clusters — the higher-conviction cross-confirmations.** **SUNPHARMA fired 5 different signals** (Blue Zone Buy, Darvas, MACD Buy, MACD Strong, Narrow CPR) — the week's cleanest technical setup, doubly interesting given its bellwether print of +27% EPS. **LAURUSLABS, LODHA, MEDANTA, VAIBHAVGBL, SOTL** all triggered 3 signals across Blue Zone + MACD combinations. **QUESS** (Blue Zone Buy + Bull Flag + MACD Strong) triggered alongside a **37.3x volume spike** — the strongest confluence of the week. **TECHM** paired Blue Zone Buy with Narrow CPR and VCP Buy — clean setup.

**Read of the mix:** MACD expanding while VCP/Blue Zone Strong contract suggests a **rotation phase** — new leaders emerging (via MACD triggers) while the prior rally leaders are consolidating rather than extending. Not a broad breakout; a hand-off.
