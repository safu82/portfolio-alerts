## 1. What Happened This Week

Nifty 50 drifted **-0.3% for the week**, closing at **24,175.65** on Friday after opening at 24,252 on Sunday's derivatives session. The tape looked quiet on the surface, but underneath was a real rotation story — **Metals and Financial Services surged into the Leading quadrant** while Auto Components flipped out of it, and the CONGLOMERATE basket ripped **+5.5%** on the back of the **SEBI clearing Jio Platforms' ~₹37,000 crore IPO** (India's largest-ever listing). The other big domestic catalyst was the **CBIC raising gold tariff valuation** mid-week, coinciding with widening physical gold discounts and swirling import-duty-cut rumours — a genuinely two-way policy setup for the jewellery/ETF complex. Meanwhile, the **RBI ended its USD-INR swap facility 3 weeks early after mobilising $73 billion**, pushing forex reserves to an all-time high of **$729.3 billion** — the quiet macro tailwind of the week.
