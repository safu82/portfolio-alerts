## 1. What Happened This Week

The Nifty 50 closed the week at **23,398.10**, down **-2.1%** from Sunday's 23,897.70 — a fifth straight weekly loss and the lowest close in this drawdown. The dominant cause was **Brent crude spiking +10.8% to $105.66** on escalating Iran-US tensions in West Asia, which triggered a classic oil-import-vulnerability sell-off across FMCG, IT, and rate-sensitive names. Compounding the pressure: the **White House confirmed a 10% temporary tariff on Indian goods** post the US Supreme Court ruling, hitting the IT sector directly (Nifty IT sub-sectors were down -4.2% for the week). The RBI's **$8 billion+ spot FX intervention** and currency-swap-driven liquidity drain to defend the rupee (which slipped past 95/USD) added a domestic-liquidity headwind on top of the global squeeze.
