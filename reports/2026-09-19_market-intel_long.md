## 1. What Happened This Week

The Nifty 50 clocked its **sixth consecutive weekly decline**, slipping from **23,398.10 on Monday to 23,346.40 on Friday** — a modest -0.2% headline number that hides a much messier week. Tuesday's session collapsed to **23,118.60** (a five-month low) as markets digested a **US Federal Reserve rate hike — the first in three years** — and **India's August inflation print accelerated**, narrowing the RBI's room to hold rates. From Wednesday onwards the tape stitched together a three-day recovery, with Thursday and Friday delivering the week's cleanest advance-decline reads. The dominant themes: rate-hike fear (Fed done, RBI next per analyst consensus of **two hikes by end-CY2026**), a **liquidity drain of ₹500 billion by the RBI** via debt sales, and the **Tata Sons banking-license rejection** that weighed on the Tata group complex.

**Laggards:** Agriculture (-4.3%), Defence (-4.2%), Trading (-3.1%), Metals (-3.0%), Conglomerate (-2.4%).

- **Conglomerate** flipped Leading → **Weakening** (RS Δ -3.3). Reliance was down -3.7% for the week and Adani group names were mixed — the Tata group weight is visible here too.
- **Metals** and **Trading** both broke from Leading — connected to the global growth wobble after the Fed hike.

The current-quarter view is **Jun 2026 results**: **507 stocks in season**, 33 still to report.

**(a) Sector aggregate.** Median EPS-YoY tables paint a genuinely strong quarter — **Logistics +81%, Textiles +49%, Chemicals +34%, Consumer Durables +32%, Banking +27%, Healthcare +26%**. Every one of the 18 major sectors is in positive median EPS growth. This is the fundamental floor under the six-week correction — the tape is falling, but the earnings underneath aren't.

**(b) Extremes (colour only).** Neuland Labs (+963%), Glenmark (+931%), and HFCL (+777%) top the beats — small-base effects, not sector signals. On the miss side, Delta Corp (-822%) and HPCL (-398%) stand out; HPCL's swing on **+27% revenue** growth points to marketing-margin compression, which is a real read-through for oil marketing companies as crude stays elevated.

**(c) Bellwether large-caps — where the sector reads live.** Critical caveat: the `earnings_calendar` shows **zero declared results in the last 10 days**. Every EPS number in the bellwether table was filed weeks ago. This week's 1d/5d price moves are **sector-rotation reactions, not fresh earnings catalysts**.

- **Reliance (-3.7% week on -22% EPS, +27% revenue)** — the biggest weight on the index is bleeding. Rev-EPS divergence is severe; the market has been penalising the earnings quality for a while. This week's price action is part of the broad conglomerate de-rating, not a fresh reaction.
- **TCS (-4.5% week on +5% EPS, +14% revenue)** — margin compression story continues, and Thursday's -3.9% one-day drop coincides with the **US House Russia sanctions bill** advancing with a 100% tariff amendment targeting India. IT is the most exposed sector to that risk.
- **HDFC Bank (+5.4% week on +18% EPS)** — the standout. On a week when the RBI drained liquidity and rejected Tata's bank license, HDFC Bank's +5.4% is a flight-to-quality trade within banking. Watch whether this decouples the largecap private banks from the sector's Weakening quadrant flip.
- **Titan (-4.4% week on +63% EPS)** — a huge print, but the market sold it. Likely explanation: **rate-hike fear on discretionary spend + the gold-price surge compressing jewellery margins**. Negative read-through for premium-discretionary. Would want the call transcript to confirm.
- **Adani Ports (+3.2% week on +2% EPS, +19% revenue)** — the price is rewarding volume growth over earnings; infrastructure quadrant flipped Improving → **Leading** this week and this print supports that.

## 9. The Bottom Line

1. **The six-week correction is showing internal repair.** Breadth improved from 40% to 46% above the 200-EMA, fresh lows collapsed from 57 to 14, and Thursday-Friday printed clean A/D expansion. But Nifty is still below all three EMAs and no new 52-week highs printed anywhere — this is stabilisation, not reversal. Reclaiming the EMA 20 at 23,684 would be the first evidence of anything more.
2. **Defensive rotation is the tell.** FMCG flipped Improving, Consumer Services flipped Leading, HDFC Bank ripped +5.4% while mid-tier banks lagged, and Titan sold off on +63% EPS. Money is going to quality and staples, not cyclicals — consistent with the rate-hike overhang. Watch whether the FMCG improvement translates to sustained price action in second-tier names or fades in a week.
3. **The earnings base is strong but the policy tape is hostile.** Every sector shows positive median EPS growth for Jun 2026. That's the floor. The ceiling is set by RBI liquidity tightening, the CPI print, the Fed cycle, and the Russia-sanctions overhang. Until one of those clears, dips will be shallow but rallies will be capped.
