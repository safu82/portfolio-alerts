## 1. What Happened This Week

Nifty 50 eked out a **+0.4% weekly gain**, closing at **22,520.45** on Friday after a brutal mid-week swing: Tuesday's rally to 22,776 was almost entirely surrendered by Thursday's crash to 22,231 (a 544-point intraday capitulation on advance-decline of 35:548), before Friday clawed back 289 points. The dominant story this week was **the aftermath of the RBI's 25 bps hike to 5.50%** (continuing from last week, but driving everything), compounded by **two fresh domestic policy jolts**: Nayara Energy's ₹5/litre petrol and ₹3/litre diesel hike with a 90-day bulk-diesel restriction, and the edible-oil import-duty cut that's expected to lift sunflower-oil imports 30%. Add surging Brent (+2.1% to $103, with mid-week spikes above $106 on US-Iran tensions) and the rupee stuck near record lows despite RBI's dollar-forward curbs, and you get the chop you saw — a textbook defensive-rotation week where **FMCG and IT led while Auto, Realty, and Conglomerates got hammered**.

## 3. Sector Rotation — Where Money Is Flowing

**Top 3 (5-day median):** FMCG **+2.1%**, Media **+1.4%**, IT **+1.0%**
**Bottom 3:** Conglomerate **-6.5%**, Realty **-3.7%**, Auto **-3.5%**

The rotation this week is **defensive-and-dollar-earners up, rate-sensitives down** — exactly what you'd expect after an RBI hike with 100 bps more guided. Three quadrant flips (RRG = Relative Rotation Graph, which maps sectors across Leading/Weakening/Lagging/Improving quadrants based on relative strength and momentum) tell the story cleanly:

- **FMCG: Improving → Leading** (RS Δ +3.0). The edible-oil duty cut is a direct tailwind for food processors, and the sector is also benefiting from defensive flows as risk assets wobble.
- **IT: Lagging → Improving** (RS Δ +4.0). A weak rupee is manna for exporters, and TCS's +15% YoY EPS print (filed earlier) is reinforcing the sector bid.
- **Realty: Leading → Lagging** (RS Δ -5.0) and **Auto: -3.5% weekly**. Both are pure rate casualties — a 5.50% repo with more hikes guided kills the EMI-sensitive buyer.

Also worth flagging: **Energy flipped Leading → Lagging** despite Brent surging — because the Nayara fuel-price hike plus the 90-day bulk-diesel restriction is being read as margin pressure for downstream refiners, not a tailwind. **Logistics flipped Lagging → Leading** (RS Δ +3.4), notable given fuel costs — likely a pricing-power read.

**Signal mix read:** this is a **contracting rally** — nearly every indicator shrank WoW, with only MACD Strong expanding. Combined with 29% of stocks above EMA200 (also contracting), the setup universe is narrowing to a handful of high-conviction names rather than broadening out.

## 9. The Bottom Line

1. **The structural trend is down, but the rotation is live.** Nifty is 6.4% below its 200-EMA with only 29% of stocks above theirs — this isn't a healthy tape. But FMCG, IT, and Logistics all flipped into stronger RRG quadrants this week, driven by concrete causes (edible-oil duty cut, weak rupee, pricing power). Worth tracking which flips sustain into next week.

2. **Policy is dominating price action — read the digest, not the charts.** The RBI hike, Nayara fuel hike, bulk-diesel restriction, edible-oil duty cut, and Scotch tariff cut all hit this week. Every top/bottom sector traces back to a specific policy catalyst. Caveat: policy-driven moves can reverse fast if the policy signal shifts (e.g., any RBI dovish surprise would flip the sector leaders).

3. **TCS set the IT bar; the real earnings season starts in 2-3 weeks.** With only 3 stocks reported and 542 pending, don't anchor on this week's prints. TCS's +15% EPS beat and clean +3.9% reaction is a positive read-through, but Infosys, HCL, Wipro, HDFC Bank, Reliance will tell the real story.
