## 1. What Happened This Week

Nifty closed the week at **23,053.10**, up **+2.0%** from the prior Sunday's 22,600 — a decisive bounce after what had been a rough October. The index clawed back on Thursday (+1.0%) and Friday (+0.5%) following a jittery Wednesday around the US Fed decision. The dominant catalysts: the **Fed held rates steady** (removing an immediate overhang, though two members hinted at a future hike), the **RBI's rupee defence** got reinforced via curbs on rupee bets, and two meaningful domestic policy moves landed — the **cotton import duty exemption was extended to December 31** (textile tailwind) and the **petrol export duty was scrapped while diesel/ATF levies were hiked** (refiner tailwind, aviation input-cost headwind — though aviation still topped the sector table on crude softening). It was a textbook "policy-plus-pause" week — the macro didn't improve so much as it stopped getting worse.

## 3. Sector Rotation — Where Money Is Flowing

**Top 3 sectors (5D):** Aviation **+7.2%**, Healthcare **+5.0%**, Media **+3.2%**.
**Bottom 3:** Agriculture **-9.2%**, ETF **-3.1%**, Consumer Durables **-1.5%**.

Aviation's rip is directly tied to **Brent falling 2.0% to $112** — fuel is ~40% of airline opex, so any crude softening flows straight to the bottom line. The caveat: the digest's new **ATF levy hike** is a partial offset, so the aviation move may not fully sustain if fuel policy bites.

Healthcare's strength shows up everywhere this week — Sanofi, Apollo Hospitals, Fortis, Astrazeneca, Thyrocare, Ajanta Pharma all appear in either rank-surge or volume-breakout tables. This isn't a one-stock story; it's sector rotation into defensives as IT (the previous leader) rolls over.

**Quadrant flips (RRG — Relative Rotation Graph, which maps sector momentum vs the index into Leading/Weakening/Lagging/Improving quadrants):**
- **IT: Leading → Weakening** (RS Δ -7.6). The biggest story of the week. Infy, TCS, HCL all beat on earnings — but the sector is -0.8% for the week and the rotation is unmistakable.
- **AGRICULTURE: Weakening → Lagging** (RS Δ -10.6). Confirmed by the -9.2% weekly print.
- **CEMENT: Lagging → Improving**. Early stirrings — don't overweight yet.

The **gold/silver divergence** worth flagging: silver fell **-6.4%** and gold **-1.0%** globally, which usually hurts domestic metal ETFs — and ETF is indeed in the bottom 3 at -3.1%. No divergence tell this week.

## 9. The Bottom Line

1. **The bounce is real but narrow.** Nifty reclaimed the 20-day EMA on the Fed pause + domestic policy tailwinds, but it's still below the 50- and 200-day. Breadth confirmed Thursday, diverged Friday. We're in a tactical rally inside a bigger consolidation — not a trend change yet.

2. **Rotation out of IT into Healthcare + Aviation is the week's cleanest signal.** The RRG flip (IT: Leading→Weakening), sector returns, bellwether action (Infy +2.7% is modest; L&T +6.2% is loud), and the multi-stock healthcare cluster all point the same direction. Watch whether this is a one-week pivot or the start of a quarter-long theme.

3. **MACD Buy surge (+10 fresh stocks) is the under-the-radar tell.** More stocks turned momentum-positive this week than any other indicator. Combined with a Bull Flag count of zero, the honest read is: individual stocks are stabilising, but the market isn't breaking out broadly. The multi-signal cluster (CYIENT, KOTAKBANK, TRENT, PVRINOX) is where confirmation is strongest — these are higher-conviction setups, not generic buy signals.
