## The Week in One Line
Nifty slipped **0.8%** to 24,366 as oil spiked, Tata leadership news weighed, and traders digested fresh RBI rules.

## What's Working
- **Defence (+2.2%)**: HAL's steady earnings (+15% profit growth) and continued government order momentum kept the sector in "leading" territory on our sector map.
- **Agriculture (+1.7%)**: A quiet bounce after weeks of underperformance — no single catalyst, more a rotation into beaten-down names.
- **Telecom (+1.6%)**: Bharti Airtel rose 2.7% on the day of results (profit +26%), and HFCL's blockbuster print (EPS up 777%) added to the buzz.

## What's Not
- **Infrastructure (-2.8%)**: Weak sentiment continues — the sector is down nearly 13% this year. Rising oil hurts input costs for builders.
- **Cement (-2.7%)**: UltraTech results were fine (+17% EPS), but the stock fell 4% over five days — a sign the market wanted more given the price it had already run to.
- **Textiles (-2.7%)**: A pullback after a strong 6-month run (+13.5%). Some profit-taking, nothing structural.

## Notable Events

**Oil got expensive again.** Brent crude jumped **4.4%** to $87 as US-Iran tensions flared. On top of that, the government hiked **petrol and diesel by ₹3/litre** and Delhi CNG by ₹2. This is a double hit: it squeezes airlines, logistics firms, and auto makers, and it eats into household budgets — which is why energy-sensitive sectors sold off.

**RBI reshaped how loans are priced.** The central bank proposed a new framework standardising how banks and NBFCs set floating-rate loan interest. It's aimed at protecting borrowers, but it also limits how much banks can tweak margins — mildly bearish for lender profits. Separately, RBI shut its **FCNR(B) window** (a special deposit scheme for NRIs) early after $52 billion rushed in — a good problem to have for the rupee.

**Tata Group's leadership shuffle.** Chairman N. Chandrasekaran's exit rattled Tata stocks and dragged the Sensex down 188 points on one session. When a conglomerate this large has succession uncertainty, it ripples across dozens of listed names.

## Charts to Know

**Breadth is quietly weakening.** Breadth simply measures how many stocks go up vs down each day. This week, declining stocks beat advancing ones on 5 of 6 sessions — and on Friday, 379 stocks fell vs just 201 that rose. The share of stocks trading above their long-term (200-day) average also slipped from 59% to 56%. Translation: the sell-off wasn't concentrated in a few big names — it was broad.

**Nifty is sitting right on its short-term trendline.** The index closed at 24,366, just barely above its 20-day moving average (a smoothed trendline of the last month's prices) at 24,349, but **below** its 200-day average of 24,405. That 200-day line is the key long-term marker — losing it decisively would be a warning sign.

## What to Watch Next Week

- **Earnings**: The bulk of Jun-quarter results are done, but watch late reporters in mid-caps. Notably strong sector medians so far: **Logistics (+81% EPS)**, **Textiles (+49%)**, **Chemicals (+34%)** — these are the sectors where fundamentals are actually improving.
- **Macro**: Any update on the US-India talks around the **Russia oil tariff bill** is the single biggest external risk. Also watch crude — every $5 up in Brent adds pressure on inflation and the rupee.
- **Nifty levels**: **24,405** (the 200-day line) is the ceiling to reclaim. On the downside, **24,165** (the 50-day line) is the first cushion. A close below that would suggest the pullback has legs.
