## 1. What Happened This Week

Nifty 50 finished the week at **24,042.85, up +1.7%** from last Friday's 23,633, with four green sessions in a row (Mon–Thu) before a mild Friday pullback. The driver wasn't subtle: the **US-Iran peace deal sent Brent crude down 9% to $80.37**, a 3-month low, which simultaneously fixed the rupee's oil-import math and lit a fire under aviation. Layered on top, the **RBI opened listed Indian equities to foreign investors directly** and **Citi flagged ~$80bn in incoming flows** — a structural bullish setup that the market took straight to the EMA20 and EMA50. The one offset was the **US Fed holding rates but signalling tighter policy ahead**, which hammered IT on Friday and explains why Nifty couldn't close above 24,180 Thursday's high.

## 9. The Bottom Line

1. **The week was a policy-and-oil rally, not an earnings rally.** Brent -9%, RBI opening equity access to foreigners, and Citi's $80bn flow call did the work. With no fresh earnings declarations and the Fed cutting against IT, the rotation is being driven by macro and flows. Watch whether these catalysts sustain — single-event rallies fade if the next data point disappoints.
2. **Financial Services is the cleanest setup on the board right now** — quadrant flip to Leading, the biggest momentum delta (+0.86), five names in the top-10 volume breakouts, and a structural FII-flow tailwind. The RBI mis-selling crackdown is a real near-term offset for private retail banks specifically (HDFCBANK reaction confirms), so the rotation may favour PSU / NBFC / capital-markets names over the obvious private-bank trade.
3. **Breadth is recovering but not yet breaking out.** 53% above EMA200 and only 1 fresh 52W high on Friday means this is still a repair tape, not a thrust tape. Nifty below the 200-EMA is the honest summary — until that breaks, treat the rally as tactical and respect the 23,654 EMA20 as the line that, if it goes, takes the whole setup with it.
