## The Week in One Line

Nifty slipped just **0.2%** to close at **24,210**, with policy tailwinds for realty and IT offsetting an oil spike and rekindled trade-tariff worries.

## What's Working

- **Realty (+4.4%)**: The RBI's steady interest rates keep home loans cheap, and property developers continue to ride strong demand. Best-performing sector for the month too (+18%).
- **IT (+2.6%)**: A rebound after a brutal 6 months. TCS's decent quarterly numbers (revenue up 14% year-on-year) helped, and the sector's quadrant improved from "Lagging" to "Improving" — meaning it's starting to catch up with the market.
- **Healthcare (+1.0%)**: Defensive buying as investors sought stable, non-cyclical earnings amid trade-tariff noise.

## What's Not

- **Textiles (-4.3%)**: The US proposed a **12.5% tariff** on Indian goods under a forced-labour probe, and textiles/apparel are directly in the crosshairs.
- **Agriculture (-4.2%)**: A stalled India-US trade deal (July 24 deadline looming) hurt export-linked agri names.
- **ETF basket (-3.6%)**: Gold ETFs pulled this basket down as global gold slipped 1.6%.

## Notable Events

**Oil jumped, and pump prices followed.** Brent crude rose **6% this week** to $76.59 as US-Iran military tensions escalated. The government hiked petrol and diesel by ₹3/litre and CNG by ₹2/litre in Delhi. That's bad for transport, logistics, and anyone selling non-essentials — households have less money left after filling up.

**Government cut import duty on electronics parts.** Customs duty was removed on select smartphone components and lithium-ion battery machinery. This is a direct boost for domestic phone assemblers and electronics manufacturers — it makes local production cheaper and supports the "Make in India" push.

**US tariff threat clouds exports.** The US proposed a 12.5% tariff on Indian goods under a forced-labour investigation. India has asked for an exemption on 1,600 items. Until this clears, textile, apparel, and labour-heavy exporters stay under pressure — which is exactly what the sector table shows.

## Charts to Know

**Breadth whipsaw.** Look at advance/decline — a simple count of how many stocks went up vs down each day. Wednesday was ugly: **523 stocks fell, only 62 rose**. But Thursday and Friday flipped — **485 stocks advanced on Friday**. That kind of swing tells you the selling was a one-day panic, not a trend, and buyers stepped in quickly.

**Nifty vs its moving averages.** Nifty is sitting **above its 20-day and 50-day** average prices (short-term uptrend intact) but **below its 200-day** average (longer-term trend still healing). Translation: the market is recovering but hasn't fully broken out yet. The 200-day line near **24,431** is the ceiling to watch.

## What to Watch Next Week

- **Earnings**: Quarterly results season just kicked off. TCS has reported (steady); watch for more IT and bank results — Infosys and HDFC Bank typically follow in this window.
- **Macro**: **June inflation data** — likely above the RBI's 4% target, which would reduce hopes of a near-term rate cut. Also watch **crude oil** if Iran tensions worsen, and the **July 24 India-US trade deal deadline**.
- **Nifty levels**: Support around **23,900** (the 50-day average). Resistance at **24,430** (the 200-day average). A clean break above that would be the first real "all-clear" signal in months.
