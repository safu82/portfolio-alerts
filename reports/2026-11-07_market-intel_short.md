## The Week in One Line
Nifty jumped **+8.6%** to 25,030 — its strongest week in months — powered by a crude oil crash and a Reliance-led earnings boost.

## What's Working
- **Aviation (+12.3%)** — Brent crude tumbled ~11% on the week, slashing jet fuel costs for airlines.
- **Conglomerate (+12.0%)** — Reliance's strong Q2 numbers lifted the whole bucket.
- **Cement (+9.2%)** — A relief bounce after months of underperformance, helped by hopes of a government growth push.

## What's Not
"Worst" is relative this week — every sector was green. These just lagged:
- **ETF (+0.8%)** — Passive baskets couldn't match the sharp sector rotation into cyclicals.
- **Industrial (+1.6%)** — Profit-taking after a strong run; capex names cooled off.
- **IT (+2.0%)** — US midterm uncertainty around H-1B visas and tariffs capped gains despite decent earnings.

## Notable Events

**Crude oil collapsed, and India loved it.** Brent crude fell roughly 11% to around $99, driven by softer global demand signals around the US midterm results. India imports ~85% of its oil, so cheaper crude means lower inflation, a stronger rupee, fatter margins for airlines and refiners, and more room for the RBI to stay growth-friendly. This was the single biggest driver of the week.

**Reliance delivered.** RIL's Q2 earnings beat expectations with EPS up 41% year-on-year — and because Reliance is the largest weight in the Nifty, its results alone pulled the index higher. The stock gained over 13% for the week.

**US midterms eased trade worries.** Headlines suggested the political shift in Washington may soften Trump-era tariff pressure on India — a tailwind for export-linked sectors like auto, engineering, and pharma. Separately, the RBI kept its FY27 growth forecast at a healthy **6.9%**, reassuring investors that domestic fundamentals are intact.

## Charts to Know

**Breadth is quietly broadening.** The share of stocks trading above their 200-day moving average — basically, the proportion of the market in a long-term uptrend — climbed from **26% last Wednesday to 38-39% by Friday**. More stocks are participating, not just the index heavyweights. That's the kind of expansion that makes rallies more durable.

**The RRG map (Relative Rotation Graph — a tool that shows which sectors are gaining or losing strength vs the broader market) flipped bullish.** Aviation rocketed from "Lagging" straight into "Leading." Conglomerate, Healthcare, Textiles, and Telecom all shifted into "Improving." Only IT and Media moved the wrong way. When six sectors improve in one week, that's rotation, not noise.

## What to Watch Next Week

- **Earnings tail:** Most Nifty heavyweights have reported, but watch mid-cap results — especially in pharma (DIVISLAB, GLENMARK, EMCURE all showed technical strength this week) and auto components.
- **Macro:** India's October **CPI inflation print** is due — a soft number would reinforce the RBI's growth-friendly stance. Also watch Brent crude: if it bounces back above $105, this week's tailwind reverses fast.
- **Nifty levels:** Support around **24,500** (this week's breakout zone). Resistance near **25,200-25,300** (prior swing high). The 20-day EMA — a short-term trend line — sits far below at 23,212, meaning the index is stretched and a pause or shallow pullback would be normal, not alarming.

**One educational note:** A +8.6% week is unusual — the long-run average weekly move for Nifty is closer to ±1-2%. Weeks like this feel euphoric, but they often cluster with higher volatility ahead (the India VIX, the market's "fear gauge," ticked up slightly). Stay process-driven.
