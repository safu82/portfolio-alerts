## 1. What Happened This Week

Nifty closed the week at **23,605.55**, down **-0.6%** from Sunday's 23,746 reference, but the headline number masks a sharper story underneath: Monday opened with a relief rally to **24,044** on the back of Brent crude collapsing **-12.3%** to $91.80 (US-Iran tensions de-escalating), only for Friday to bleed **-301 points** on a brutal A/D split of 136 advancers vs 448 decliners. The dominant catalysts were a mix of external relief and domestic policy: Brent's slide, the new RBI Governor's "rupee may be undervalued" comments, the **cotton import duty waiver** (textile-supportive), and the lingering **15% gold import duty** that continues to weigh on Titan. The week also produced ten sector quadrant flips — meaning the rotation is happening in real time, not theoretically.

## 2. The Trend Picture

Nifty is **below all three of its key EMAs (Exponential Moving Average — a smoothed average of recent closing prices used as a trend reference)**:
- Below **EMA 20** (23,835) by -1.0%
- Below **EMA 50** (23,940) by -1.4%
- Below **EMA 200** (24,577) by -4.0%

That's a clearly bearish trend structure — the index is now ~4% under its long-term trend line, and the EMA 20 sits below the EMA 50, confirming the medium-term setup has rolled over. Breadth tells a more nuanced story though: **% of stocks above the 200-EMA improved from 42% to 45% over the week**, peaking at 49% mid-week. Monday (May 25) was the standout — **440 advancers vs 145 decliners**, a textbook stealth-strength day. Friday undid most of it. **New 52-week lows surged to 12 on Friday**, the worst single-day reading of the week. So: index trend bearish, but internal breadth is mixed-to-improving, not collapsing — a classic divergence that needs the next week to resolve.

## 3. Sector Rotation — Where Money Is Flowing

**Top performers** were **Auto Components (+3.5%)**, **Telecom (+3.0%)**, **Industrial (+1.9%)** and **Banking (+1.9%)**. The bottom: **Agriculture (-5.8%)**, **Logistics (-4.1%)**, **Conglomerate (-2.1%)**.

The big story is the **quadrant flips on the Relative Rotation Graph (a framework that maps each sector's momentum and relative strength against the benchmark, sorting them into Leading / Weakening / Lagging / Improving quadrants)**:

- **Banking flipped Lagging → Leading** (RS Δ +5.5) — the most significant rotation of the week. The new RBI Governor's measured tone and the dollar-rupee swap auction supported sentiment.
- **Aviation flipped Improving → Leading** — direct beneficiary of Brent's -12.3% collapse (fuel = ~30% of airline opex).
- **Auto Components** topped the table at +3.5%, again a Brent + input-cost story.
- **Telecom flipped Weakening → Leading** — interesting given Bharti Airtel's reaction (see Section 4).
- **Healthcare flipped Leading → Weakening** (RS Δ -4.5) and **ETF flipped Leading → Weakening** (RS Δ -3.1) — money is rotating OUT of the recent defensive winners.
- **Agriculture flipped Weakening → Lagging** — worst sector by 5D return.

Worth flagging: the gold import duty story is **continuing background**, but the divergence still matters — global gold was flat (+0.2%) yet domestic jewellery names like Titan are facing margin pressure from the 15% landed-cost hike. That's a policy-driven domestic story, not a global-flow story.

## 5. Technical Signals This Week

The Blue Zone universe (the dashboard's institutional-accumulation watchlist — stocks meeting momentum, EMA20 trend and proximity-to-52W-high criteria) **contracted from 54 to 42 unique stocks (-12)**. So the accumulation theme is narrowing.

- **Blue Zone Buy**: **42 vs 54 (-12)** — contraction. Top examples: LAURUSLABS, ADANIENT, BSE, HFCL, BHEL.
- **MACD Buy** (Moving Average Convergence Divergence — momentum oscillator turning positive): **37 vs 21 (+16)** — strong expansion. Examples: HONASA, TRITURBINE, POWERINDIA, ASIANPAINT.
- **MACD Strong**: **30 vs 22 (+8)** — examples: HFCL, HONASA, OFSS, ADANIPOWER, VBL.

- **Blue Zone Strong**: 4 vs 6 (-2) — SOLARINDS, TRITURBINE, SHILPAMED, AUROPHARMA.
- **VCP Buy** (Volatility Contraction Pattern — Minervini's tightening-range-before-breakout): 3 vs 6 (-3) — METALIETF, FORTIS, APARINDS.
- **Darvas Box**: 2 new — ADANIPOWER, SCHNEIDER.
- **Pullback Bounce**: 1 — RADICO.
- **Golden Cross Buy** (20-EMA crossing above 50-EMA): 1 — MINDACORP.

**Multi-signal cluster stocks** — these are the higher-conviction setups firing 2+ different indicator types: **TRITURBINE (4 signals)** is the standout, hitting Blue Zone Buy + Strong + Bull Flag + MACD Buy simultaneously. **HONASA, PAGEIND, VBL, WOCKPHARMA, NAVINFLUOR, FLUOROCHEM** all fired 3 signals each. **ADANIENT** (Blue Zone Buy + Narrow CPR) and **ADANIPOWER** (Darvas + MACD Strong) round out the Adani-pair confirmation.

The signal mix is **rotating, not expanding**: MACD and Narrow CPR are surging (momentum building under the surface), while Blue Zone and VCP are contracting (the existing leaders are narrowing). Classic mid-rotation behaviour.
