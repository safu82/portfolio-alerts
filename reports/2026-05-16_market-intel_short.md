## The Week in One Line
Nifty fell **2.1%** to **23,659** as rising oil and West Asia tensions triggered a broad risk-off slide.

## What's Working
- **ETF (+4.2%)** — Gold and silver ETFs caught a safe-haven bid as nervous investors rotated out of stocks.
- **Telecom (+4.1%)** — Bharti Airtel held up post-earnings; Tata Communications saw a notable rank surge.
- **Healthcare (-0.2%)** — Defensive sector cushion: pharma names like Zydus, Max Healthcare and Abbott outperformed the broader fall.

## What's Not
- **Realty (-8.9%)** — Worst hit. Rising oil prices and weaker risk appetite hammered rate-sensitive realty stocks hardest.
- **Trading (-7.6%)** — Broker and exchange stocks fell as falling markets typically dent trading-volume optimism.
- **Defence (-6.5%)** — Sharp profit-booking after a strong run; HAL slipped 8% over 5 days despite OK earnings.
- **IT (-6.1%)** — Bellwethers TCS, Infosys and HCL all lost 5%+ even on decent results, as global growth fears weighed.

## Notable Events

**Oil spike + West Asia tensions.** Brent jumped **+5.2%** to **$107**. Higher crude is bad news for India — it widens our import bill, pressures the rupee, and squeezes companies that use fuel as a raw input. This was the single biggest driver of the week's slide.

**Mid-week reversal then fade.** Tuesday saw an ugly washout — only **36 stocks rose vs 548 that fell**. Thursday bounced 350 points on bargain-hunting, but Friday gave some back. Classic shaky-bottom price action, not a clean reversal yet.

**Mixed earnings season.** Mar-2026 results are mostly solid: **Metals (+47% EPS), Financials (+26%) and Healthcare (+19%)** are leading. But large-caps disappointed in *price reaction* — Reliance fell 6.9% post-results, Titan -7.5%, HAL -8.4%. Numbers were fine; the market simply isn't rewarding them right now.

## Charts to Know

**The Nifty trend ladder is broken.** Nifty (23,659) is now below its 20-day, 50-day **and** 200-day moving averages — these are simply running-average lines that smooth out price. When price is below all three, the short, medium and long-term trends have all flipped negative. That's a caution flag.

**Breadth is mixed, not panicked.** "Breadth" = how many stocks rose vs fell. After Tuesday's nasty 36-vs-548 wipeout, advances recovered to 348 on Thursday — but only **43% of stocks** are still trading above their 200-day average (down from 57% last week). Translation: damage is real but not catastrophic.

## What to Watch Next Week

- **Earnings due:** Roughly 212 stocks haven't reported yet for Mar-2026. Watch mid-cap industrials and any straggler banks for confirmation that earnings strength is broad-based.
- **Macro events:** Brent crude — if it pushes past $110, expect more pressure. India VIX (the "fear gauge") is at **18.6** and rising; above 20 signals real nervousness. Also watch the rupee and US Dollar Index (DXY at 99).
- **Levels on Nifty:**
  - **Resistance ~23,890** (the 20-day average) — bulls need to reclaim this to call the dip over.
  - **Support ~23,400** (Tuesday's low) — a break below opens the door to deeper losses.

**One quick concept:** A **"quadrant flip from Leading to Weakening"** (which happened to **Banking, Energy, Financials, Infrastructure, Realty** this week) is a simple way of saying these sectors were the market's strong horses, but are now losing speed. Doesn't mean crash — means leadership is changing. **ETF flows** (gold, silver) becoming the new "Leading" group fits the cautious mood.

Stay patient. Trend is down, but earnings are decent and breadth hasn't collapsed. Weeks like this are for watching, not chasing.
