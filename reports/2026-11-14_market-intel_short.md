## The Week in One Line
Nifty gained **+0.6%** to close at **25,144**, as cheaper oil and strong FII inflows offset a nasty inflation surprise.

## What's Working
- **Media (+5.1%)** — Strong Sep quarter earnings (median EPS +31% YoY) finally turning sentiment on a beaten-down sector.
- **Trading (+4.7%)** — A bounce-back rally after months of underperformance; MMTC saw a 21x volume spike.
- **Chemicals (+4.1%)** — Multiple blowout earnings (LXCHEM, GUJALKALI both +400%+ EPS) plus an RRG quadrant flip from Lagging to Improving. *RRG = a rotation chart; "Improving" means a sector is finally gaining strength vs the market.*

## What's Not
- **ETFs (-3.5%)** — Likely reflects weakness in gold/silver ETFs after global precious metals fell ~4% on a stronger US Dollar.
- **Textiles (-0.5%)** — US tariff threats (reportedly up to 500% on cotton) spooked exporters.
- **IT (-0.3%)** — Bellwethers like TCS, Infy and HCLTech all slipped 3-4% over five days despite decent earnings; global tech jitters continue.

## Notable Events

**Inflation ran hot — and that's a problem.** Retail inflation hit **6.1%** in October, breaching the RBI's 6% upper limit for the first time in two years. Economists now expect the RBI to hike rates by up to 50 bps during FY27. Banks have already raised deposit rates. That's bad news for rate-sensitive sectors like real estate, utilities, and NBFCs — but curiously, Banking still rallied **+3.8%** on the week, likely on the GDP upgrade (see below) and strong Sep earnings from HDFC Bank, ICICI, SBI and Axis.

**RBI raised its FY27 GDP forecast to 7.1%** (from 6.9%). A rare bullish counter-signal to the inflation worry — it says growth momentum is strong enough to absorb tighter policy. This supports banks, industrials and autos.

**Oil kept falling.** Brent dropped another **3.1%** to $96. That's a direct tailwind for aviation (IndiGo +4%), paints, tyres and oil marketing companies — all of which use crude as a raw material or fuel.

## Charts to Know

**Breadth tells a cautious story.** On Friday, decliners (387) sharply outnumbered advancers (193) — a reversal after four strong days earlier in the week. *Breadth = how many stocks went up vs down; it tells you whether a rally is broad-based or narrow.* Meanwhile, the share of stocks trading above their 200-day moving average climbed from 39% to 41-45% through the week — slow, grinding improvement rather than a surge.

**Technical "buy" signals jumped sharply.** Stocks firing a **Blue Zone buy** (a trend-following entry setup) doubled from 17 to 36. **Golden Crosses** (when the 50-day average crosses above the 200-day — a classic long-term bullish signal) rose from 23 to 37. This hints at building momentum beneath the surface, even as the index itself looks flat.

## What to Watch Next Week

- **Earnings:** Most Nifty 50 bellwethers have already reported. Watch mid-cap chemical, auto-component and defence names for continuation of the earnings beat trend.
- **Macro:** Any RBI commentary on the inflation breach — explicit hike signals could pressure banks and real estate. Also watch oil (any reversal above $100 flips the script) and the India-US tariff deal progress.
- **Nifty levels:** Support at **24,980** (last week's low and near the 20-day EMA at 23,975 as a deeper cushion). Resistance at **25,300** (Thursday's high). A break above 25,300 opens room higher; a fall below 24,980 suggests the inflation story is winning.
