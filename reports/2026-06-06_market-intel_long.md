## 1. What Happened This Week

Nifty closed the week at **23,368.25**, down **-1.0%** from the prior Sunday's 23,605.55 — a soft, rotational week dominated by Friday's **RBI policy decision**. The MPC held the repo rate steady at **5.25%**, cut its FY27 growth forecast to **6.6%**, and raised the inflation projection by 50 bps to **5.1%** — a stagflation-tinted message that markets didn't love. Layered on top: **Brent crude jumped 4.0% to $95.49** on US-Iran tensions, **FY27 Nifty earnings estimates were cut 9%**, and a fresh **US proposal of a 12.5% tariff on India over forced-labour concerns** rattled export-facing sentiment. Offsetting the gloom, **RBI simplified FPI (Foreign Portfolio Investor — overseas institutional buyers of Indian securities) rules for government bonds** and **India-Oman CEPA went live** — both quietly bullish.

## 2. The Trend Picture

Nifty is **below all three key EMAs (Exponential Moving Average — a smoothed average of recent closes used as a trend reference)**: -1.3% under the 20-day (23,679.75), -2.0% under the 50-day (23,850.21), and -4.8% below the 200-day (24,541.07). That's a textbook **downtrend stack** — short, medium, and long-term trends all pointing the same way.

Breadth confirms the cautious tape. **Only 44% of stocks are above their 200-day EMA**, drifting in the low-40s all week. Tuesday (2026-06-02) was the strongest day with **367 advances vs 217 declines** — a clean 1.7:1 split, the only proper up-day of the week. Concerning: **fresh 52-week highs were zero every single session**, while 52-week lows ranged 10–18 daily. So the market isn't crashing — it's grinding, with leadership scarce and damage accumulating quietly in the laggards.

## 5. Technical Signals This Week

**140 unique stocks fired at least one signal this week.** The breakdown:

- **MACD Buy (momentum oscillator turning positive — a trend-change signal)**: jumped from **37 to 50 unique stocks (+13)**, with 34 fresh entries. The biggest expansion this week. Examples: **HFCL, HONASA, BALAMINES, THYROCARE, SYRMA**. This is the broadest "early-momentum-turning-up" signal we have, and it's clearly expanding.
- **Narrow CPR Breakaway (Central Pivot Range — a tight pivot setup that often precedes breakouts)**: **51 stocks vs 46 prior (+5)**. Examples include **ADANIENT, ZYDUSLIFE, ASIANPAINT, MOTHERSON, PIDILITIND** — quality large-caps coiling.
- **Blue Zone Buy (the dashboard's institutional-accumulation watchlist — momentum stocks with RSI strength, above 20-EMA, near 52W highs)**: **41 vs 42 (-1)** — essentially flat. Examples: **IDEA, LAURUSLABS, HFCL, ADANIENT, POLYCAB**.
- **MACD Strong**: 28 vs 30 (-2). **LAURUSLABS, HFCL, RRKABEL** standing out.
- **VCP Buy (Volatility Contraction Pattern — Minervini's tightening-range-before-breakout setup)**: jumped from **3 to 7 (+4)**, including **POLYCAB, GRASIM, BANDHANBNK**.
- **Blue Zone Strong**: 5 vs 4 (+1). **ADANIENT, SOLARINDS, BALAMINES** the standouts.
- **Bull Flag Buy (sharp rally → orderly consolidation → breakout above the range)**: dropped **9 → 5 (-4)** — only one fresh entry. **The clearest sign breakouts are getting harder to come by.**
- **Pullback Bounce (strong-trend stocks dipping to EMA20 and recovering)**: 4 vs 1 (+3). **VBL, RADICO, ANGELONE** — consumption-staples and broker plays.
- **Golden Cross Buy (20-EMA crossing above 50-EMA)**: 4 vs 1 (+3). **MINDACORP, SUPRAJIT** confirm the auto-components leadership flip.

**Multi-signal cluster stocks (higher-conviction setups firing 2+ different indicator types):** **BALAMINES** (4 signals — both Blue Zones plus both MACDs) leads the list. **ADANIENT** (Blue Zone Buy + Strong + Narrow CPR), **HFCL** (Blue Zone + both MACDs), **THYROCARE** (Blue Zone + Bull Flag + MACD), **CRAFTSMAN** (Bull Flag + both MACDs), and **SYRMA** (Blue Zone + both MACDs) are the names worth watching.

**Signal mix read:** MACD expanding broadly + Bull Flag contracting + Pullback Bounce rising = **early-stage rotation, not late-stage breakout.** Money is flowing into setups that are *forming*, not stocks that are already running.

## 9. The Bottom Line

1. **The trend is down but the rotation is constructive.** Nifty below all three EMAs and -1.0% for the week, but 13 sectors flipped into Leading, MACD signals expanded by 13 stocks, and Autos + Energy + Media are showing genuine rotation. The damage is at the index level; the opportunity is sector-specific. Caveat: rotation in a downtrend often fails — needs Nifty to reclaim the 20-EMA to validate.

2. **The RBI-rupee bind is the dominant macro overhang.** A rate hold with downgraded growth + upgraded inflation, against a falling rupee, against $95 Brent, against US tariff noise — that's a heavy combination. Until either Brent cools or the rupee stabilises, large-cap defensives and rate-sensitive sectors stay challenged. FMCG and Financial Services bottoming this week's tables is consistent with that.

3. **Watch Autos, Media, and PSU banks for follow-through; treat Cement and Consumer-discretionary retail with caution.** Auto Components and Automobile turning Leading on fundamentals (M&M, Maruti, Titan-like reads) is the most credible rotation story. Cement's UltraTech-led collapse and Consumer Services' Shoppers Stop / ABFRL / Sapphire EPS misses are the clearest red flags — sector signal and individual prints aligned, which is rare.
