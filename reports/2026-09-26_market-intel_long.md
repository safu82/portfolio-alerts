# Weekly Market Report — Week Ending 26 September 2026

## 1. What Happened This Week

Nifty 50 slipped from **23,346 to 23,140**, a **-0.9% weekly loss**, but that headline understates the damage — the index printed a brutal **-1.6% single-day drop on Thursday** (23,447 → 23,063) before a limp Friday recovery. The dominant catalyst was **Brent crude pushing above $105/bbl (+1.7% WoW)**, which battered financials and insurers directly through the IRDAI proposal-plus-oil combo, and reinforced the seven-week slide in Indian equities. Compounding the pain: the **RBI conducted ~$10 billion in FX swaps** and drained ₹71,971 crore in liquidity to defend a rupee under pressure from DXY strength (**101.21, +0.90 WoW**), while **India's forex reserves fell $14.88 billion to $765.9 billion**. A fresh geopolitical overhang — **Trump signing the Russia sanctions bill with power to slap 100% tariffs on India** — sat heavily on IT and pharma exporters all week.

## 2. The Trend Picture

The trend is unambiguously **broken across all timeframes**. Nifty at 23,140 sits **below its EMA20 (23,543, -1.7%), below its EMA50 (23,812, -2.8%), and below its EMA200 (24,224, -4.5%)** — an Exponential Moving Average is a smoothed average of recent closing prices used as a trend reference, and when price is below all three, the medium-term posture is defensive.

Breadth confirms the damage. Only **41% of stocks are above their 200-day EMA** by Friday (down from 45% earlier in the week), and **Thursday's tape was ugly** — just **86 advancers against 496 decliners**, with **29 fresh 52-week lows** and zero new highs. Friday's near-even 290/292 A/D split was the "exhausted seller" bounce, not accumulation. Notably, across the entire week there was only **one new 52-week high** — that's a market where leadership has evaporated, not one taking a breather.

## 9. The Bottom Line

1. **The tape is fully broken; the fundamentals are not.** Nifty is below all three key EMAs and 41% of stocks are below their 200-day, but Jun-quarter earnings prints show +18–27% EPS growth across banks and financials. Confirmation needed via second-tier names and volume.

The multi-signal cluster (WELSPUNLIV, RBLBANK, SYRMA, CASTROLIND) is where cross-indicator conviction sits. Treat single-signal names with more caution than usual.
