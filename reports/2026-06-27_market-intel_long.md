## 1. What Happened This Week

Nifty 50 ended the week barely changed at **24,056** (+0.1%), but the flat tape masks a genuinely interesting week underneath. The dominant catalyst was **Brent crude collapsing 7.6% to USD 74.23**, driven by US-Iran diplomatic easing and Hormuz de-escalation — a textbook bullish setup for India, the world's third-largest crude importer. Layered on top: the RBI flagged it **may revise FY26 growth above 7%**, sweetened FCNR(B) NRI deposit rates to ~7%, and opened domestic bank lending against those deposits — a triple-shot of dollar-inflow and credit-channel positives. The kicker, though, is what *didn't* hold: Tuesday's sharp -1.2% drop to 23,795 wiped out Monday's gap-up, and the week ended in a low-volatility consolidation rather than a clean breakout.

## 3. Sector Rotation — Where Money Is Flowing

The rotation this week was unusually clean and tied directly to crude.

**Top 3 (5-day median):** Aviation +8.7%, Textiles +1.8%, Healthcare +1.6%
**Bottom 3:** Trading -8.4%, ETF -8.0%, Metals -4.1%

**Aviation's +8.7% rip** is straight off the crude move — fuel is 30-40% of an airline's cost base, and INDIGO showing up on the volume breakout list at **4.5x average volume** is the rotation happening in real time. **Textiles and Auto Components** also benefited from a second catalyst: India is pushing for **preferential market access in the US trade deal** (July 24 deadline) — both sectors are highly tariff-sensitive exporters.

Quadrant flips were broad and bullish: **10 sectors moved into better quadrants** this week — Infrastructure, Consumer Services, Chemicals and Industrial all jumped from Lagging/Weakening straight to **Leading** (Leading = sectors in the Relative Rotation Graph's top-right quadrant, showing both strong relative strength and improving momentum). This kind of broad-based quadrant migration is what an early rotation looks like.

The standout puzzle is the **ETF sector down 8.0%** for the week and **-15.6% over the month** — given that "ETF" in this dataset is dominated by gold/silver instruments, this aligns with **silver -11.7% and gold -3.1%** in USD this week. No divergence story here; precious metal ETFs simply tracked the global selloff as the safe-haven bid unwound on Hormuz easing.

## 9. The Bottom Line

1. **The rotation is real but unconfirmed.** Ten sectors flipped to better quadrants, Blue Zone expanded to 62 stocks, and Aviation/Textiles/Auto Components are leading on identifiable catalysts (crude, trade deal). But Nifty is still below its 200-day EMA, MACD Strong contracted from 29 to 13, and new 52-week lows still outnumber new highs. Treat this as **early rotation, not breakout** — confirmation requires Nifty over 24,455 with breadth above 55% over EMA200.
2. **RBI policy is doing more for banks than earnings are.** Banking's median +9.9% EPS is the weakest in the table, yet PSU banks (FEDERALBNK, BANKBARODA, MAHABANK) are clustering in VCP setups and SBIN held green on a -3% print. The catalyst is the FCNR(B) rule + 7% growth signal, not the P&L — worth watching, but a thinner foundation than earnings-led leadership.
3. **The IT disconnect is worth respecting.** TCS and Infosys posted strong prints (+12%, +24% EPS) and got sold (-4.9%, -7.7% over 5 days). That's the market saying it doesn't trust the forward story — trade-deal uncertainty, US demand questions. Until US-India trade clarity lands, IT remains a sector to observe rather than chase.
