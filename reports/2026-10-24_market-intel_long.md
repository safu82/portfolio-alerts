## 1. What Happened This Week

Indian equities bled all week — Nifty 50 slid from **23,146.15 on Sunday to 22,600.00 by Friday**, a **-2.4%** cut with declines on every single session. The dominant cause is clear from this week's events digest: **Brent crude surged +8.7% to $114.37/bbl**, and the RBI Governor publicly signalled openness to using **policy rates as a financial-stability tool** to defend a sliding rupee — a double shock of imported inflation and the threat of a hike into a weak economy. Layer in a fresh RBI Bulletin flagging **broad-based food price increases**, and you get textbook risk-off: breadth collapsed, bellwether earnings were ignored, and the only genuinely bullish catalyst — the **India-New Zealand FTA** going live Oct 20 — barely registered against the crude move.

This isn't a mild pullback — it's a broad-based de-rating.

## 9. The Bottom Line

1. **The sell-off is macro-driven, not earnings-driven.** Bellwether EPS prints across Banking (HDFCB +5%, Axis +39%), FMCG (Nestle +36%), and Industrials (BHEL +118%) were strong — and the market ignored every one of them because crude +8.7% and an RBI rate-hike signal reset the discount rate for the whole market. Honest caveat: the "Recent Earnings Declared" window was empty, so these prints are OLD; the real test comes when Dec-quarter names start reporting.

2. **IT is the one shelter worth watching.** TCS +7.7%, HCLTECH +4.6%, INFY +4.0%, TECHM +8.5% over 5 days, plus a cluster of golden-cross and MACD signals in the sector — a rupee-weakness tailwind play. Confirmation needed via a continued move above sector EMAs; this breaks if the rupee strengthens meaningfully from Jio inflows.

3. **The breadth deterioration demands caution before calling a bottom.** 147 fresh 52-week lows on Friday vs 13 the prior Friday, pullback-bounce signals at zero, and four leadership sectors flipping Leading → Lagging in one week is not a one-day capitulation pattern — it's an ongoing distribution. Watch for an advance/decline split that actually prints >1.0 before trusting any bounce.
