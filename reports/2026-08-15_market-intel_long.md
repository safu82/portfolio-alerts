## 1. What Happened This Week

The Nifty 50 lost **0.8% this week**, closing at **24,366.00** on Friday after starting Monday at 24,583.80 — a slow, five-day bleed rather than a single-day flush. Two catalysts did most of the damage: **Brent crude jumped 4.4% to $87.09** on renewed US-Iran tensions and the US Senate's Russia sanctions bill (which threatens 100% tariffs on Indian buyers of Russian crude), and the **Tata Group leadership transition** — with N. Chandrasekaran exiting his role — dragged conglomerate names lower. Domestically, the **RBI's proposed new floating-rate loan framework** (standardising rate-setting across banks and NBFCs) added a bearish overhang to lenders, while a fresh **₹3/litre petrol-diesel price hike** kept transportation-linked sectors under pressure. The offsetting bright spot was defence, which held its ground on continued rotation strength.

The **Energy weakness (-1.6%)** is worth parsing carefully: Brent rose 4.4%, which normally lifts upstream refiners, but the **US Senate sanctions bill on Russian crude buyers** is a specific overhang for Indian refiners (HINDPETRO, BPCL, Reliance). ## 9. The Bottom Line

1. **The tape is rotating, not trending.** Nifty is pinned between its 20-EMA and 200-EMA, breadth is narrowing (56% above 200-EMA vs 59% mid-week), and the signal mix shows Blue Zone accumulation broadening (+20 stocks) while MACD momentum thins (-19 stocks). Mid-cycle behaviour — not a top, not a fresh leg up.

2. **Defence and Consumer Durables are the cleanest rotation stories.** Defence had a quadrant flip to Leading with confirming action across HAL, BEL, BDL. Consumer Durables flipped to Leading with Titan's +2.3% and LALPATHLAB's Blue Zone + VCP Strong combo. Both worth watching for follow-through — caveat: rotations can round-trip inside two weeks if breadth doesn't confirm.

LICI's +5.0% suggests the safer read is in insurance / non-lending financials until the framework's specifics clarify.
