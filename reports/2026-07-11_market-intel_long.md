# Weekly Market Report — 2026-07-11

## 1. What Happened This Week

Nifty 50 finished the week at **24,210.85**, a slim **-0.2%** dip from Sunday's 24,270.55 — but that headline masks a wild ride. The index printed a mid-week gut-punch on **Wednesday, closing at 23,896.30** (down ~1.9% intraday), before staging a two-day recovery through Friday. The dominant catalyst was **US-Iran military tensions escalating** — Brent Crude jumped **+6.1% to $76.59** — combined with a **domestic petrol/diesel price hike of ₹3/L** (CNG up ₹2/L in Delhi) that landed mid-week and stung transport, logistics and discretionary names. Cushioning the blow: the **CBIC's customs-duty exemption on electronics, smartphone parts and Li-ion machinery inputs** (bullish for EMS/electronics), and the **UK FTA vehicle quota notification** (tariff benefits kick in July 15). Under the hood, the real story was **rotation**: IT flipped from Lagging to Improving, FMCG woke up, while Aviation, Infrastructure and Textiles got dumped hard.
